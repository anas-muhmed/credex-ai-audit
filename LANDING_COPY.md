# Landing Copy

This is the copy for the landing/form page. Every line was written against a specific insight from user interviews — not generic SaaS marketing language.

---

## Headline

**AI Spend Audit**

Short and literal. The ICP (founders, engineering managers) scans quickly and decides in under 3 seconds. "AI Spend Audit" tells them exactly what this is. No clever wordplay — Sinan described closing tabs when the value proposition was unclear upfront.

---

## Subheadline

**Find out where your team is overpaying for AI tools — free, instant, no sign-up required.**

Three reassurances in one line, each addressing a real friction point from user interviews:
- "free" — removes the paywall concern before it forms
- "instant" — addresses the "I don't have time for this" objection
- "no sign-up required" — directly answers the trust concern both Sinan and Dharaneesh raised independently

---

## CTA Button

**Run my audit →**

"My" makes it personal. "→" suggests forward movement, not just a click. Avoids generic "Submit" or "Get started" language that signals a funnel rather than a tool.

---

## Form hint text (below the tool input)

**Include tools you subscribed to but rarely use**

This line came directly from Dharaneesh (Interview 2) — he had a forgotten auto-renewal on a tool he used exactly once. The hint prompts users to enter tools they might otherwise exclude because they feel embarrassed to admit they are paying for something they barely touch. Those are often the highest-savings entries.

---

## Results page CTA (shown when savings > $500/month)

**Get these savings guaranteed**

Not "Book a call." Not "Talk to sales." "Guaranteed" is the operative word — it implies Credex takes accountability for the outcome, not just the conversation. The subtext: "A Credex advisor will audit your stack and implement the savings for you."

CTA button: **Book a Credex consultation →**

Full width on mobile, generous padding. Impossible to miss. Blue — same color as the form submit button so the visual language is consistent throughout the flow.

---

## Social proof / trust signals

No testimonials exist yet — this is a new product. The trust signals used instead:

1. **Transparent pricing sources** — each tool's pricing data links to the vendor's official pricing page. Users can verify the numbers themselves. Sinan specifically said pricing source transparency is what builds trust in a recommendation tool.

2. **Specific reasoning per tool** — every recommendation shows a "reasoning" line tied to the user's actual seat count and use case, not generic advice. Dharaneesh said "cancel everything and use free plan" type recommendations feel untrustworthy. Specific reasoning feels credible.

3. **No email required to see results** — the audit runs first. The email form appears below the results. This design choice is itself a trust signal — it signals the tool is not a lead capture trap.

## FAQ

**Is this actually free?**
Yes. No credit card. No trial period. The audit tool is permanently free. Credex makes money from consulting engagements, not from the tool itself.

**Do you store my data?**
Your audit results are saved to generate your shareable URL. Email is only stored if you choose to provide it. No data is sold or shared with third parties.

**How accurate are the pricing numbers?**
Every number traces to an official vendor pricing page, verified in May 2026. Links are in the audit results. Prices change — if you spot an error, the source URL lets you verify directly.

**Why does it recommend switching tools?**
Recommendations are based on your actual seat count and use case — not generic cost-cutting advice. If the math does not make sense for your team, ignore it. The reasoning is shown so you can judge for yourself.

**What happens if my savings are above $500/month?**
You will see an option to book a Credex consultation. A Credex advisor will review your stack and help implement the savings. There is no obligation to book.

---

## What was deliberately cut

- "Save thousands per year" — sounds like urgency marketing. Sinan's exact words: "'you are wasting $5000 per year' type nonsense is an immediate red flag."
- Testimonials — no real ones exist, fake ones would destroy trust if discovered
- Feature list — nobody reads feature lists before trying a free tool
- Pricing page — there is no pricing, the tool is free
